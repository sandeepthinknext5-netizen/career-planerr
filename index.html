WE'RE HERE TO HELP

Questions aspirants
ask us

Choosing where to begin is easier when you have the right information. Here are a few things students often ask.

Which courses are available at Career Planner?

Career Planner offers preparation for SSC, Banking, Railway, CET, CAT/MBA, Defence and other competitive examinations. Contact the team for current batch details.

How can I choose the right course for my exam?

Tell us which exam you are interested in and our team can guide you to the relevant course and preparation path.

Where are Career Planner centres located?

Career Planner has centres in Patna, Muzaffarpur and Samastipur. Check the Branches section for individual centre details.

Can I enquire about current batches?

Yes. Call 9386347918 or use the enquiry form to ask about upcoming batches, schedules and course availability.

How can I speak with a counsellor?

Call 9386347918, email careerplannerindia@gmail.com or send an enquiry using the consultation form below.
